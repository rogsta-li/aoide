# AOIDE

Aoide is a self-hosted music library management application designed to organize, enrich, and maintain personal digital music collections. It combines comprehensive metadata management, automatic file organization, and artwork retrieval with detailed information about artists, albums, and individual tracks. Personal ratings, favorites, playlists, and music discovery features help users manage and explore their collections.

The name **Aoide** (Ancient Greek: Ἀοιδή) derives from the Greek word for *song* or *chant*. In Greek mythology, Aoide was one of the original Muses and the personification of song and voice. The name reflects the application's focus on music, its organization, and the discovery of musical works.
